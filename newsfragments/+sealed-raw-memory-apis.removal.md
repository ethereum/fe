APIs that read or write memory through a raw pointer, read or write storage at a numeric slot, build a safe abstraction from raw parts, or expose uninitialized typed contents are now `unsafe fn`, and calling them requires an `unsafe` block or function. This covers `core::ptr::{copy, copy_raw, zero_bytes, alloc_array}`, `MemArray::new_uninit`, `MemSlice::from_raw_parts`, `MemBuffer::{capacity_span, set_len}`, `core::abi::{store_word, encode_field_at}`, the `from_abi_tail` constructors of `DynString`, `Bytes` and `DynArray`, the `RawMem` and `RawStorage` methods, `stor_ptr`, `tstor_ptr` and `calldata_ptr`, the `at` constructors of `SolSlot`, `SolArray`, `SolEnumerableSet` and `SolMapping` (whose `slot` fields are now private), `std::native::Args::new` and `std::evm::random::shuffle_slice`. The trait methods `Encode::encode`, `ByteInput::copy_to_memory`, `ByteInput::copy_to_memory_unchecked`, `StorageKey::write_key` and `EncodePacked::write_packed` are `unsafe fn`, and their implementations must be too. Binding an effect provider that reaches its target through a raw pointer, such as `with (p)` for `p: *T`, also requires an `unsafe` context.
