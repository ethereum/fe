Encoding and decoding no longer trust what a type's safe hooks report. `EncodePacked::write_packed` takes the `room` its caller reserved and reverts with `Panic(0x01)` rather than write past it, so a `packed_width()` that answers differently on a later call, alone or inside a tuple, cannot make `keccak_packed` or `encode_packed` overrun their buffer. A payload end that an `AbiSpan` implementation reports past the input is a decode error.
