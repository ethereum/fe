A string literal passed to a generic `String<N>` parameter, such as `width("abc")` for `fn width<const N: usize>(_ s: String<N>) -> usize`, now infers `N` from the literal instead of failing with "type annotation is needed". `N` is the literal's length unless something else fixes it. A length that cannot be shown to hold the literal is rejected, including a generic one such as `M` or `{M + 1}`, which a too-short instantiation used to turn into an internal compiler error.
