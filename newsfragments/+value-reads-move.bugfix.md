A non-`Copy` value read moves wherever an expression is evaluated as a value, through any wrapper and any access: an `if` or `match` join of one owner, a block-wrapped `match` or `if let` scrutinee, a wrapped `mut self` receiver or callee, a block passed to a view parameter and a producer reused by a second loop no longer copy the owner, which let safe code write through a view or hold two owners of one buffer. A loop holds any other non-`Copy` driver by a view of its place for the whole loop, and a cast that unwraps a newtype reads the field it reaches rather than the whole value.
