Added revert and returndata helpers to `std::evm`: `bubble_revert_or` (bubble a failed call's revert data, or revert with a custom error when there is none), `encode_error`, `revert_bytes` and `revert_empty` for raising, `revert_selector`, `has_selector`, `is_error`, `decode_error_string` and `decode_panic` (with Solidity `catch Error(string)` / `catch Panic(uint)` acceptance rules) for inspecting revert data, and `ReturnData`, a `ByteInput` over the current returndata buffer so it can be read or ABI-decoded in place without copying, or copied once into `Bytes` with `ReturnData::to_bytes`.
