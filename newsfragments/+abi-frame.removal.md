`Encode::encode` writes into a `core::abi::AbiFrame` instead of through a raw pointer. An implementation takes `_ out: AbiFrame` and writes with the frame's `store_word`, `copy_from` and `slice` methods, or with `encode_field_at` for a field of a head/tail layout. Each write is checked against the frame, so implementing and calling `Encode` needs no `unsafe`, and an encoding that writes more than its `AbiSize` reports panics instead of overwriting the next value. The encodings derived for `msg` variants, `#[abi]` structs and `#[error]` types contain no `unsafe` code, so encoding a dependency's ABI types does not require `allow_unsafe`.
