A call's arguments, and an assignment's value, may now write the receiver or the target's root, and the call or the store sees what they wrote: `h.set(reset(mut h))`, `self.total = self.bump()` and `v[0] = v.bump()` are accepted; moving the receiver is still rejected. A receiver is read when its access opens after the arguments, so a `Copy` field receiver (`l.pair.sum(l.bump())`) no longer sees its value from before them, and an owned field receiver's arguments may read it (`l.inner.consume(l.len())`). An assignment to a projection of a temporary, such as `make()[0] = value()`, evaluates `make()` before the value.
