Preserve parseable, stable formatting of wrapped operators, qualified types, and chained expressions.

`fe fmt` no longer deletes comments placed among an item's attributes or among a module's inner attributes, and keeps a blank line after a module's inner attributes.

`fe fmt` no longer takes exponentially long on deeply nested tuples.

`fe fmt` keeps a `uses` clause on the line of a long function signature without a body, where a line break made it fail to parse.
