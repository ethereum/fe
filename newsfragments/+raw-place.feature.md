`#[raw_place]` declares a projection with nothing to resume after its yield: no code runs there, it keeps no access or session of its own open across it, and it yields no place of its own frame. The body is checked against the declaration where it is written (16-0014), and an unsafe split's access components are now places or calls of `#[raw_place]` projections (8-0118), since an ordinary projection may keep accesses for code after its yield that one split session cannot merge. The std raw-slot constructors (`SolArray::at`, `StorageMap::at`, `StoragePackedArray::at` and the others) and `core::effect_ref::target_mut` are declared `#[raw_place]`, so libraries can add split components of their own.
