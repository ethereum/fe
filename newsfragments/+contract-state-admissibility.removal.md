Contract state holds only what outlives a transaction. A storage or transient field may not hold a memory value, any type with a raw memory pointer such as `MemBuffer` or `MemArray` (3-0065), or a persistent collection inside transient storage, such as `TSlot<StorageMap<K, V>>` or a `TStorPtr` to a struct holding a map, whose entries would persist after the transient value holding them is gone (3-0066).
