Effect handles are removed: `core::effect_ref::{EffectHandle, EffectHandleMut, AddressSpace, read, write, target_mut}`, the `read` and `write` methods of `StorPtr` and `TStorPtr`, and user-defined handle types. A `with` provider is a place: provide a pointer's target with `with (mut *p)` or `with (*p)` instead of `with (p)`, and read or write it with `*p`. A transient contract field is a `TSlot<T>`, accessed as `field[()]`, rather than a `TStorPtr<T>`.
