Safe memory APIs no longer let a size or a count corrupt other allocations. `MemBuffer::with_capacity` and `reserve` (and so `Packed::with_capacity` and `TextBuilder::with_capacity`) revert with `Panic(0x41)` above `2**64 - 1` bytes, as Solidity's allocations do, instead of returning a buffer that overlaps later allocations. `keccak_packed` and `encode_packed` revert with `Panic(0x01)` when a value's `pack_into` writes more than its `packed_width()` instead of writing past the buffer.
