`std::evm::storage_at<T>(slot)` and `storage_at_mut<T>(slot)` are the generic raw-slot constructors: `unsafe`, `#[raw_place]` projections that yield the `T` at a storage slot as a storage place, to read with `RawStorage` authority or to read and write with `mut RawStorage`. A getter that only reads raw storage therefore declares only read authority. The std collections' `at` constructors (`StorageMap::at`, `SolArray::at` and the others) are built on `storage_at_mut`, and a library's own storage collection can use either in place of writing out a `StorPtr` dereference.
