`std::evm::storage_at<T>(slot)` is the generic raw-slot constructor: an `unsafe`, `#[raw_place]` projection that, given `RawStorage` authority, yields the `T` at a storage slot as a storage place. The std collections' `at` constructors (`StorageMap::at`, `SolArray::at` and the others) are built on it, and a library's own storage collection can use it in place of writing out the `StorPtr` and `target_mut` steps.
