Moving a non-`Copy` value out of an effect or out of storage is now rejected; such reads were treated as copies, so `fn f() -> MemBuffer uses (b: MemBuffer) { b }` could hand out a second owner of `b`'s allocation. A pattern on a place now moves only what its names bind: a `match` that binds nothing leaves its scrutinee whole, and a plain payload binding through a view or a `mut` parameter is checked as a move out of that access.
