Added closures: `|x| x + 1`, `|acc: own, x| acc + x`, `|x: u256| -> bool { .. }`. A closure captures by copy or move and implements the callable shape of its parameter modes, written as a bound like `F: Fn(Self::Item) -> bool` or `F: FnMut(own A, Self::Item) -> A`; unannotated parameters take their modes and types from the bound of the parameter the closure is passed to, as in `xs.fold(0, |acc, x| acc + x)`. The effects a closure's body uses are its row `F::E`, provided where the higher-order function calling it is called. See `docs/language/closures.md`.
