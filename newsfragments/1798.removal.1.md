Storage layouts change: narrow struct fields share slots the way Solidity packs struct members; `StorageMap` values use their type's native layout, and `WordRepr` and `std::evm::word` are removed; narrow signed values are stored as their own two's-complement bytes, though old sign-extended words still read back correctly; and storage and transient fields are numbered apart. Storage collections are no longer `Copy` handles and lose their `SALT` parameters: a collection takes its slot from the contract field that holds it, so pass collections as `mut` or view parameters instead of by value. Layout holes are removed: drop `= _` defaults, `_` arguments and const parameters that only stood for a slot. Effect handles are removed: `EffectHandle`, `EffectHandleMut`, `core::effect_ref::{AddressSpace, read, write}`, and the `read` and `write` methods of `StorPtr` and `TStorPtr`. Provide a pointer's target with `with (mut *p)`, and declare a transient field as a `TSlot<T>`, read and written as `field.value`.
