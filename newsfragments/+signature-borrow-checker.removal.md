The borrow checker no longer models raw memory: dereferences of raw pointers, which now require `unsafe`, are not checked for aliasing or initialization. Some programs the previous checker accepted are now rejected: a method may only return borrows derived from `self`; a field or element of a value keeps all of that value's borrows alive; dynamic indices are assumed to overlap; facts about integer and boolean values no longer separate borrows; and raw pointers cannot point to values that hold borrows, such as `*mut u256` or `MemArray<ref T>`.
