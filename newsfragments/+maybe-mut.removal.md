The read and `mut` method pairs of `core` and `std` are now single `mut?` methods: `IndexMut` is removed (bound `mut Index<I>` and give `index` a `mut` mode instead), `MemBuffer` and `FixedMemBuffer` `span_mut`, `MemBuffer::slice_mut`, `MemArray::as_mut_slice`, `MemSlice` `at_mut`, `split_at_mut`, `try_split_at_mut` and `from_raw_parts_mut`, and `Mutex::value_mut` become `span`, `slice`, `as_slice`, `at`, `split_at`, `try_split_at`, `from_raw_parts` and `value`, taken at `mut` where their use needs it (`let s = mut buf.span()`). `MemArray::get_mut` becomes `get` and `FixedStack::top_mut` becomes `top`.
