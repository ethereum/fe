Inside a trait, an omitted `Self` default in a trait bound now stands for the bounded type. With `trait Combine<Rhs = Self>`, `fn f<T: Combine>()` in a trait means `T: Combine<T>`, and `type A: Combine` means `A: Combine<A>`; both used the enclosing trait's `Self` before. A `Self` written in a qualified path's trait arguments inside a trait, as in `<Self::A as Combine<Self>>::Out`, now stays the trait's `Self` instead of becoming `Self::A`.
